# A new home for Spectrum

Spectrum Hub is the central home for Spectrum bringing together the foundations, guidance, resources, tools, and offerings that help teams create with Spectrum. This first release establishes a new experience for exploring Spectrum across platforms. It brings together foundational guidance, design and component resources, live implementations, and support in one connected place, creating a foundation for Spectrum to grow beyond documentation. As the Hub evolves, it will become the place to understand what Spectrum is, what it offers, how to use it, and where it’s going.

### Available now

Foundations and content guidance Updated guidance for the principles and building blocks of Spectrum Component design and implementation guidance Design guidance with component availability and React Spectrum and Spectrum Web Components resources for building with Spectrum Live component playgrounds Explore and interact with components in real implementations Support Contact us to provide feedback, report bugs, or send general inquiries

### What's next

Spectrum Hub will continue to expand as the central experience for Spectrum. Future releases will bring mobile documentation, unified experience guidance, patterns and principles, new ways to explore Spectrum, and resources that support emerging ways of designing and building. Over time, the Hub will connect Spectrum’s offerings across platforms and make it easier to discover not only what exists today, but what’s possible with Spectrum.
