# In-product word list

## Using this resource

Consistent language builds user trust and strengthens their knowledge that a product is meeting their needs. Using inconsistent vocabulary across Adobe’s apps and products can be confusing to users, and it makes it harder for those creating education and documentation. Below is a list of words that we either recommend using, or suggest avoiding. Some words are noted as internal-only jargon at Adobe. We’ve still included these in this list because, while we may use them internally when we work on or talk about our products, they occasionally appear in user-facing content in ways that aren’t clear, intuitive, or appropriate. For capitalization guidance, view the Grammar and mechanics page for the UX writing style guide. Keep in mind that some terms are branded and follow Adobe Brand Guidelines for styling and usage. This list is regularly updated and is by no means complete. If you have words you’d like to submit for consideration, please fill out this suggestion form. The Adobe Design Content Strategy team reviews suggestions once a quarter, and will get in touch if they need more information.
